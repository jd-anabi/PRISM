# PRISM — saving the fit as it goes (the training side): Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A fit is saved at the end of every pass when its rows are in a kept simulation set, a stopped fit is kept as a record of its own (`fit`), and it is resumed by hand, in a new process, into the same fit (same validation rows, optimizer state, pass count, best loss, early-stopping count, best weights), only while nothing it was trained with has changed.

**Architecture:** sbi 0.26.1 stays the trainer. `core/SBI/train.py` splits the training call into `prepare_fit` (the trainer and its rows) and `run_fit` (sbi's own `train`), wraps the trainer instance's per-pass summary (`_summarize_epoch`) to hand a snapshot to a saver, and restores a snapshot onto a fresh trainer in the order the reader tested before calling `train(resume_training=True)`. A new `core/SBI/fit_state.py` holds the kept fit's files (two generations written atomically inside the cancel-deferred section), its operating-system lock, its identity (rows, settings, a digest of the named code, the resolved constants, the libraries, the network's shape), the record's lifecycle (`FitSession`) and the listing (`kept_fits`). The store gains the kind `fit` (`Artifacts/fits/`), written like the simulation set (no writer; `write_fit_manifest`), protected while it runs, protecting what it trains on. `build_posterior` and `tsnpe_round` gain `save_fit` and `resume_fit`; the tool's `train` and `tsnpe` gain `--resume-fit`.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12, torch 2.9.0+cu130, sbi 0.26.1, nflows 0.14, PySide6 6.9.3 (Records only), pytest; the conda env `biophys-env`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-fit-saving-design.md` (approved by the owner 2026-10-21, nothing struck). Every task cites the sections it implements; they bind except where "Departures from the design" below says otherwise. The evidence is `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-06-window-redesign/fit-saving/recon-report.md` (file:line for every claim; the tested restore; the sizes and times) and its scripts in `C:\Users\J\AppData\Local\Temp\prism-redesign-scratch\fit\` (`m2_resume_phase.py` is the restore that matched an uninterrupted fit five times out of five). ## For the owner: what this plan does, in plain words

**What you have at its end.** Every training whose simulation set is kept (the window's always is, and the command line's is by default) saves itself at the end of each pass, in about a tenth of a second at the retrain's size. If the program ends during a training — a crash, Windows restarting, a power cut, the window closed, Cancel, Ctrl-C — at most the pass in progress is lost, and the training is kept as a record of its own, listed in Records under "Unfinished trainings" with how far it got and how it stopped. You resume it from the command line (`python -m core train --resume-fit <its id> --bounds … --prior …`); it reads its simulation set back (about four minutes at full size) and continues the same training. It refuses, saying what differs, if a setting, the network's shape, the code that builds or trains the network, a library or its simulation set is not what it was. When it finishes, the posterior is written as today (its record now also says whether it was resumed, in how many processes and how long the fit took in all) and the kept training is removed. A narrowing round is saved and resumed the same way. A training that was never interrupted trains exactly as today.

**What it does not do yet.** The window does not offer Resume, does not ask "Keep or Discard" after Stop, and its words about Cancel are unchanged: those come with stages 3, 4 and 6. Until then Cancel in the window keeps the training without asking (nothing is lost), and Records lists and deletes kept trainings in today's form.

**How big it is.** Six building tasks and two I run myself. Expect it to grow by two or three as trial runs and reviews find things, as the last stages did: about 15 to 25 million tokens at the care you chose.

**What needs your hand.** Nothing until the exit. At the exit: a quiet machine for the fast gate of record (about 17 minutes) and for the slow set (about an hour and a half); the card gates (the smoke runs as `CLAUDE.md` has them, plus one new run: a training ended like a power cut after its third pass and resumed); and two yes-or-no answers: the new sentence about the fit in `CLAUDE.md`, and the new run in the card gate's block.

**What I will do on your computer beyond editing code and running tests.** Tiny trainings on the processor in the tests (seconds each) and on the card in the fast gate's card tests and the card gates. No training of real size; nothing under your real `Artifacts/` is touched.

**Choices in this plan that you have not seen** (the full list with costs is "Departures from the design" below):
- A kept training has no name of its own in Records: it shows "(unnamed <id>)", what posterior it is for, how many passes it made and its state. The posterior's name and note travel inside it.
- Resuming takes every setting from the kept training itself; a setting you type that differs is refused, naming it. A narrowing round resumes in the region it was trained in.
- The states a kept training reads: "running", "stopped unexpectedly", "kept when stopped", "cannot be resumed" (with why) and "finished" (its fit ended but the program stopped before the posterior was written; resuming writes the posterior at once).
- The card gate's new run uses a small script in `tests/` that ends the training like a power cut right after a chosen pass is saved, because nothing outside can stop a fit of a few seconds at an exact pass.

**What does not change.** No simulation, feature, simulation set's identity or what a row holds (`Artifacts/simulations/eda5c2a1e468` stays usable); sbi stays the trainer and its method, network, early stopping and best-weights rule are unchanged; no window page beyond Records' list of kinds; `CLAUDE.md` is edited only on your yes at the exit.

## Review Focus

Conditions the design implies that a person will meet and that no task's tests would otherwise exercise; each line's test is in the task that owns the code.

1. **The same kept training resumed twice at once** (the tool in one shell while the window or another shell runs it): the second is refused before it reads a single row, saying a running process holds it, and Records and the listing read "running": Task 5, `test_a_kept_training_another_process_holds_is_refused_and_listed_as_running` (with Task 1's lock test across processes).
2. **A disk that fills, or a file a scanner holds, partway through a days-long fit:** every failed pass warns with the pass the kept training stands at, the fit goes on to its posterior, and a training stopped after failed saves resumes from its last good pass: Task 4, `test_a_save_that_fails_warns_and_the_training_goes_on`; Task 5, `test_a_training_whose_later_saves_failed_resumes_from_its_last_good_pass`.
3. **A crash in the instant between a save's two renames, or a newest state a scanner left unreadable:** the resume continues from the pass before it, says so, and never reads half a snapshot: Task 1, `test_a_save_torn_at_each_of_its_steps_leaves_the_last_good_pass_readable`; Task 5, `test_a_kept_training_whose_newest_state_is_unreadable_resumes_from_the_pass_before_it`.

- `constants(names: tuple[str, ...] = FIT_CONSTANTS) -> dict` — `getattr(core.config, name)` as it stands, tuples as lists.

- `libraries() -> dict[str, str]` — `{"torch": torch.__version__, "sbi": importlib.metadata.version("sbi"), "nflows": importlib.metadata.version("nflows"), "sbi_trainer_files": trainer_files_digest()}`.
- `network_digest(net: torch.nn.Module) -> str` — sha256 of `"\n".join(f"{name}:{tuple(t.shape)}")` over `net.state_dict()` sorted by name, 16 hex.
- `indices_digest(indices: torch.Tensor) -> str` — sha256 of the int64 bytes, 16 hex.
- `class FitMismatch(Exception)` — a check made once the rows are read found the kept fit cannot continue; `str(e)` is a sentence fragment naming what differs.
- `@dataclass(frozen=True) class Difference`: `part: str`, `name: str`, `kept: object`, `now: object`.
- `first_difference(kept: dict, now: dict, parts: tuple[str, ...] = IDENTITY_PARTS) -> Difference | None` — parts in order; a part absent from `now` is skipped; a dict part is compared name by name (the kept dict's order, then names only `now` has), a scalar part (`batches`, `network`) whole with `name` = the part; values compared as `training_checkpoint._canonical` makes them (floats by `repr`, tuples as lists).
- `write_header(path: Path, header: dict) -> None` (`atomic_torch_save`); `read_header(path: Path) -> dict`; `save_state(path: Path, state: dict) -> None` (inside `runs.cancel_deferred()`: `atomic_torch_save(state, path / STATE, previous=path / STATE_PREV)`); `load_state(path: Path) -> dict | None` (`state.pt`, else `state.prev.pt`, each with `map_location="cpu", weights_only=False`; None when neither loads; the dict gains `"_state_file"`).
- `class FitLock`: `__init__(self, path: Path)` (the record's folder); `acquire(self) -> bool` (opens or creates `path / LOCK` and takes a non-blocking lock on its first byte: `msvcrt.locking(fd, msvcrt.LK_NBLCK, 1)` on Windows, `fcntl.flock(fd, LOCK_EX | LOCK_NB)` elsewhere; keeps the handle open; False when held); `release(self) -> None` (unlock and close; idempotent); `held: bool` (property).
- `running(path: Path) -> bool` — True when any open handle holds the record's lock (this process's included; the lock is per handle): a fresh handle tries the lock and lets it go at once. False when the lock file is absent. Never raises.
